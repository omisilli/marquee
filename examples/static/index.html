marquee

A strip that scrolls round without a seam. The list is written once; the package copies it beside itself, as many times as the window needs, and moves it by one list's length, round and round. Mouse over one: it slows to a stop. Tab into one: it stops, so a link can be read. No dependencies.

The browser's own scroll With Lenis

1 · Flow — the announcement bar

Round and round, at a speed in px a second

--pg-marquee-speed is px a second, not a time: a longer list goes round at the same speed. --pg-marquee-hover: under the mouse it pauses, slows, or goes on. The button at the end stops it and goes on — anything with data-pg-marquee-toggle and aria-controls; moving for more than five seconds, a strip should have one.

3 · Logos — two rows, the opposite ways

The edges fade: --pg-marquee-fade

One row left, the other right (--pg-marquee-direction: right); the edges fade out over 6rem. Each list is as long as it is: the window is filled with as many copies as it needs.

--pg-marquee-drag: on: grab a row — a finger or the mouse — and drag it along; let go and it is thrown on, easing back to its own speed. A finger going across it still scrolls the page. The switch drags the logos, the testimonials below and the deals above (a swipe there is the next deal, or the one before).

4 · Columns — testimonials going up and down

Up or down: a column with a height of its own

--pg-marquee-direction: up or down, each column its own speed. Going up or down the window needs a height, the site's — here 28rem — and is best written with data-pg-marquee-axis="y" already, so it is a column from the first paint. Below 48rem, one column. Mouse over a card and press its link: the copies are pressed as the list is.

Under the mouse, the column stops — or all three: data-pg-marquee-group on what holds them, and the mouse anywhere over it, or focus in it, stops every marquee inside. A toggle naming the group stops and starts them all.

4 · Rows — reviews beside a headline

The same, sideways: cards side by side

Two rows of review cards beside the score, the opposite ways, the edges fading. A long review ends with … after four lines, and its Read more opens the whole of it in a popup — the rows stand still while it is open (pauseMarquee), and go on when it closes. Under the mouse it stops, a card's product link can be pressed — in the copies too — and with ?drag=on a finger throws it along. Each product picture sits in its box the house way (pg-imageGroup): the box has the height and the shape, the picture fills it.

5 · Overflow — only when it does not fit

A long title moves, a short one stands still

--pg-marquee: overflow: while the list fits the window it stands still, with no copies (data-pg-marquee-fits); when it does not, it flows. Narrow the window: more titles start to move.

6 · With the page's scroll — two bands crossing

Faster as the page goes down, turning back as it goes up

--pg-marquee-scroll: the page's scroll speed, px a second, times this number, is added to the flow — scroll down hard and the bands race, scroll up and they turn back. --pg-marquee-turn: keep: having turned, they keep that way until the page goes down again. No GSAP: the package reads the scroll itself, Lenis or not. The bands are plain CSS, rotated.
